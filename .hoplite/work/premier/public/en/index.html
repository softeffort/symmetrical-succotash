FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Payments, limits and plans in short

Is it really free?
Yes. The Free plan is free forever and needs no card. It has daily limits on messages, Python runs and websites — enough to try the service properly.
When do the limits reset?
Every day at midnight Moscow time. A regular question uses one message. Complex tasks where the model works in several steps (searching, running code, building a site) use one message per step.
Is there auto-renewal?
No. You pay once for the term you choose — one month or several months at once with a discount. Nothing is charged automatically. Renewing adds days to your current term.
How can I pay?
By card or SBP, with international Visa and Mastercard, or with crypto via @CryptoBot. Available methods are shown in the payment window.
What happens when my plan ends?
You go back to Free. All chats and files are kept, and the Premier badge returns on your published websites.
Can I switch plans?
Yes, at any time — the remaining days are recalculated for the new plan.
What is the difference between plans?
Limits and models. Paid plans give more messages, Python runs and websites per day, all models and publishing without the Premier badge. The cloud computer is included in Pro and Max. See the plan cards above for details.
Can I use Premier in Cursor, Codex or Claude Code?
Yes, via the API. Your key and setup guides are on the developers page.
How do I contact support?
Message us on Telegram: @royesee — we help with payments, limits and anything else.
